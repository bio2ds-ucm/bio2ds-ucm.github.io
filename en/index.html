the group

Data science for biomedicine

We are the BIO²DS research group (Biomedical Data Science & Biostatistics), officially based at the Faculty of Statistical Studies of the Complutense University of Madrid.

Our work focuses on the analysis of data in the fields of medicine, health and its social determinants, omics and biology, under the umbrella of data science, Machine Learning and biostatistics. The group is open to all kinds of collaborations with researchers, predoctoral and postdoctoral fellows, and students.
