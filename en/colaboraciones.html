External collaborations

We collaborate as researchers and instructors with national and international centres in statistics, bioinformatics, biomedicine and Machine Learning.

  • SUNlab — Network Science Institute, Northeastern University (Boston).
  • Sirota Lab — Bakar Computational Health Sciences Institute, UCSF (California).
  • Nephrology and Kidney Transplantation — Vall d'Hebron Hospital (Barcelona).
  • Tumor Microenvironment and Immunotherapy — Hospital 12 de Octubre (Madrid).
  • Cardiovascular Pathophysiology — Spanish National Centre for Cardiovascular Research (Madrid).
  • Medical Oncology Department — Hospital Clínico San Carlos (Madrid).
  • Neurovascular Research Unit — University Institute for Neurochemistry Research, UCM.
  • Master's in Bioinformatics and Data Science in Personalized Precision Medicine and Health — National School of Public Health, ISCIII. Teaching.
  • Statistical models and analytics applied to Big Data — Madrid City Council training programme. Teaching.
